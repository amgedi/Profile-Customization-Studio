# Assets and licences

PCS source uses AGPL-3.0-only. This does not relicense photographs, icons or dependency code.

The two photo manifests (`apps/studio/public/photos/licenses.json` and `apps/studio/src/photo-licenses.json`) are intentionally duplicated: one is public metadata, the other is imported by app/export code. Keep them identical. Each entry records title, creator, source URL, licence URL, attribution and modifications. Historical retrieval dates are not known and are not invented.

CC BY assets need creator/source/licence credits and modification notices. CC BY-SA assets also retain their own share-alike obligations for image adaptations. Public domain/CC0 entries do not make every other asset CC0. Original PCS branding/procedural scenes are project artwork. No third-party anime background collection is bundled.

[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) includes current image credits. Export packages generate attribution for used bundled photos; preserve those files/links when publishing. User imports are rights-unverified until their owner supplies metadata.

All current bundled source pages were checked during publication preparation. Credits identify the original file; cropped/resized local files remain adaptations under the recorded licence. New assets need verified redistribution rights before inclusion. Keep required native icon copies at their existing embed paths even when hashes match.
